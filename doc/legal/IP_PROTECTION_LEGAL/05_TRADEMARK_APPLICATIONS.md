## FILING PROCESS

### Step 1: Online Filing (RECOMMENDED)

**Website:** https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark

1. Create an account at the UK IPO
2. Complete online application for each trademark
3. Pay filing fee (credit/debit card)
4. Receive application number immediately

### Step 2: Paper Filing (Alternative)

**Form:** TM3 (Trade Mark Application Form)
**Address:**
```
Intellectual Property Office
Concept House
Cardiff Road
Newport
South Wales
NP10 8QQ
```

### Step 3: Examination Process

**Timeline:**
- **Week 1:** Application received and assigned number
- **Week 2-4:** Formalities check (corrections may be requested)
- **Week 4-12:** Examination by IPO examiner
- **Week 12-16:** Publication in Trade Marks Journal (2 months opposition period)
- **Week 16-24:** Registration certificate issued (if no opposition)

**Common Issues:**
- Descriptive marks (e.g., "Music Distribution Co") may be rejected
- Similarity to existing marks
- Insufficient description of goods/services

## INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION (OPTIONAL - YEAR 2)

After UK registration, consider international protection:

**Madrid Protocol (International Registration):**
- File through UK IPO for international coverage
- Cost: £700 base fee + fees per country
- Recommended countries: USA, EU, Canada, Australia

**EU Trademark (EUTM):**
- Single registration covers all 27 EU countries
- Cost: €850 (approx. £730)
- File through EUIPO: https://euipo.europa.eu

**US Trademark (USPTO):**
- File directly with USPTO: https://www.uspto.gov
- Cost: $250-$350 per class
- Classes 9, 35, 41, 42 same as UK

## TRADEMARK USAGE GUIDELINES (POST-REGISTRATION)

Once registered, use trademarks correctly:

**Correct Usage:**
- MSC & Co® (after registration - use ® symbol)
- "MSC & Co is a registered trademark of AUDIOMSC LTD"
- Consistent capitalization and spelling

**Incorrect Usage:**
- Don't use as a verb ("MSC & Co'd the song")
- Don't pluralize ("MSC & Cos")
- Don't modify ("MSC and Company")

## ENFORCEMENT

If someone infringes your trademark:

1. **Cease and Desist Letter** (£500-£1,500 via solicitor)
2. **Negotiation** (licensing or settlement)
3. **Legal Action** (£10K-£50K+ for litigation)

**Pro Tip:** Most infringement cases settle with a cease and desist letter.
