## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, whether oral, written, electronic, visual, or in any other form, including but not limited to:

(a) Software source code, object code, algorithms, and technical architecture
(b) Technical specifications, documentation, and system designs
(c) Business plans, financial projections, and revenue models
(d) Customer lists, user data, and market strategies
(e) Trade secrets, know-how, and proprietary methodologies
(f) Product roadmaps and development plans
(g) The music taxonomy system (genres, moods, instruments, validation standards)
(h) Any information marked "CONFIDENTIAL", "PROPRIETARY", or with similar legend
(i) Any information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

**1.2** "**Purpose**" means the Receiving Party's evaluation of a potential investment in, acquisition of, or strategic partnership with the Disclosing Party.

---

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

**2.1** The Receiving Party hereby agrees to:

(a) Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
(b) Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose stated above
(c) Not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party
(d) Apply at least the same degree of care to protect the Confidential Information as it applies to its own confidential information (and in no event less than reasonable care)
(e) Not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code of any software
(f) Not use the Confidential Information for any competitive purpose
(g) Return or destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies) upon request by the Disclosing Party
(h) Disclose the Confidential Information only to those of its employees, advisors, and representatives who have a genuine need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those in this Agreement

**2.2** The Receiving Party acknowledges that disclosure may be limited to specific individuals approved in writing by the Disclosing Party.

---

## 3. SPECIFIC RESTRICTIONS

**3.1** The Receiving Party shall NOT:

(a) Implement, replicate, or create derivative works based on any disclosed systems, methodologies, or business processes
(b) Use the music taxonomy, genre classifications, enum structures, or validation methodologies
(c) Share the Confidential Information with portfolio companies, partners, or affiliates without explicit written consent
(d) Recruit, solicit, or hire any employees, contractors, or advisors of the Disclosing Party
(e) Make copies of the Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose
(f) Use any ideas, concepts, or know-how contained in the Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose
(g) File patent applications, trademark applications, or other intellectual property applications based on the Confidential Information

**3.2** If the Receiving Party is a venture capital firm, investment fund, or similar entity, it shall not share the Confidential Information with any portfolio companies without the express written consent of the Disclosing Party.

---

## 4. EXCEPTIONS

**4.1** This Agreement does not apply to information that:

(a) Was publicly known at the time of disclosure
(b) Becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party
(c) Was rightfully in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records
(d) Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records
(e) Was rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation

**4.2** The burden of proving that any information falls within these exceptions rests with the Receiving Party.

---

## 5. REQUIRED DISCLOSURE

**5.1** If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order, or government authority to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall:

(a) Promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing (unless prohibited by law)
(b) Cooperate with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain a protective order or other remedy
(c) Disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information which is legally required
(d) Use reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that the disclosed information will be treated confidentially
