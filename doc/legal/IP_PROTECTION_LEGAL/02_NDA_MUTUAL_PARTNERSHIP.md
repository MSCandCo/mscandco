---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**"), whether before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form, including but not limited to:

(a) Technical information (software, source code, APIs, architectures, specifications)
(b) Business information (plans, strategies, customer lists, pricing, financial data)
(c) Product information (roadmaps, features, development plans)
(d) Intellectual property (patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets)
(e) Any information marked "CONFIDENTIAL" or that would reasonably be considered confidential

---

## 2. MUTUAL OBLIGATIONS

**2.1** Each Party (as Receiving Party) agrees to:

(a) Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
(b) Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose
(c) Not disclose to third parties without prior written consent
(d) Protect with at least the same care as own confidential information (minimum: reasonable care)
(e) Disclose only to employees/advisors who need to know and are bound by similar obligations
(f) Not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software
(g) Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon request or termination

**2.2** Both Parties acknowledge that they are subject to equal obligations under this Agreement.

---

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

**3.1** This Agreement does not apply to information that:

(a) Was publicly known before disclosure
(b) Becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement
(c) Was rightfully possessed prior to disclosure (with written evidence)
(d) Was independently developed (with written evidence)
(e) Was rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality restrictions

**3.2** Burden of proof for any exception rests with the Receiving Party.
