## 1. Problem Statement

### Industry Challenges

#### 1.1 Copyright Confusion & Legal Exposure
- 67% of independent artists lack legal knowledge for copyright compliance
- Average copyright dispute costs £25,000-£75,000
- 23% of releases have undetected sample/interpolation issues
- No affordable pre-release copyright verification exists

#### 1.2 Environmental Impact Invisibility
- Music streaming generates 200,000+ tons CO2e annually (UK alone)
- 0% of distribution platforms show environmental impact
- Artists and labels have no carbon tracking tools
- No accessible carbon offsetting options exist

#### 1.3 Accessibility Barriers
- 15% of global population has disabilities
- <5% of music content has accessibility features
- Professional accessibility services cost £2,000-£10,000 per release
- No WCAG compliance tracking for music platforms

#### 1.4 Data Isolation & Research Barriers
- Industry data locked in proprietary systems
- Academic researchers lack access to streaming data
- Policy decisions made without empirical evidence
- No standardized open datasets exist

#### 1.5 Knowledge Gap & Skill Barriers
- 89% of independent artists lack formal music business training
- Professional courses cost £3,000-£15,000
- No AI-powered personalized tutoring exists
- Certification systems are expensive and inaccessible

### 2.2 Sustainability & Carbon Tracking Dashboard

**Innovation**: First music platform with integrated streaming carbon calculation + offset purchasing

**Technical Approach**:
- DIMPACT 2024 carbon calculation methodology (industry standard)
- Real-time streaming analytics integration
- Platform-specific carbon intensity factors
- Regional grid carbon intensity data
- Integration with carbon offset providers (Greenspark, Ecologi, Gold Standard)

**Carbon Calculation Formula**:
```
Carbon (kg CO2e) = Streams × 0.055 kWh × Grid Factor (0.233 kg CO2e/kWh)
```

**Features**:
- Release-level carbon footprint tracking
- Artist sustainability profiles with badges
- One-click carbon offset purchasing
- Auto-offset with monthly budgets
- Carbon-neutral certification
- Public sustainability sharing

**Impact Metrics**:
- Carbon tracked: 5,000 tons CO2e/year
