# MSC & Co Brand Slogan Guide

## Overview
This document provides the official brand slogans for MSC & Co and their appropriate usage across all platforms, communications, and materials.

---

## 1. MASTER BRAND SLOGAN (Primary)

**"Empowering Artists. Protecting the Planet."**

### Usage:
- Website hero
- Pitch deck cover
- All marketing materials
- Social media bio
- Email signatures
- Marketing email templates
- General company communications

### Why it works:
- Clean, balanced dual mission
- Equally weighted (not "artists first, planet second")
- Professional yet passionate
- Translates globally
- Memorable and ownable

---

## 2. ARTIST-FOCUSED VARIATION (Warmer)

**"Empowering Every Artist. Protecting Our Planet."**

### Usage:
- Artist onboarding
- Community communications
- Gospel outreach
- Personal emails
- Artist-focused campaigns

### Why it works:
- "Every" = inclusive, no gatekeeping
- "Our" = collective ownership, community
- More personal/emotional connection
- Same structure, warmer tone

---

## 3. INVESTOR/TECHNICAL (EIC Pitch)

**"Intelligent Distribution. Independent Artists. Zero Carbon."**

### Usage:
- EIC application
- Investor decks
- B2B partnerships
- Technical documentation
- Professional presentations

### Why it works:
- Three concrete benefits (not abstract values)
- "Intelligent" = AI without saying AI
- "Independent" = target market precision
- "Zero Carbon" = measurable outcome
- Professional, data-driven tone

---

## 4. GOSPEL SUB-BRAND (YHWH MSC)

**"Kingdom Music. Kingdom Impact."**

### Usage:
- YHWH MSC branding
- Church partnerships
- Gospel artist outreach
- Festival of Life materials
- Faith-based communications

### Why it works:
- Biblical language ("Kingdom" = Matthew 6:33)
- "Impact" works spiritually + environmentally
- Rhymes/memorable
- Speaks directly to faith community
- Avoids secular language

---

## 5. UNIVERSAL PERSONAL (Conversational)

**"Your Music. Your Truth. Your Impact."**

### Usage:
- Apollo AI responses
- User dashboard
- Onboarding flows
- Social media captions
- Artist testimonials
- Personalized communications

### Why it works:
- Second person = direct relationship
- "Truth" = blockchain transparency (metaphorical)
- "Impact" = carbon + cultural influence
- Empowering personal ownership
- Works in Apollo chat: "Welcome! Your Music. Your Truth. Your Impact."

## Implementation Notes

- **Default Slogan**: Use "Empowering Artists. Protecting the Planet." unless context-specific guidance indicates otherwise
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent slogan usage within the same communication channel
- **Flexibility**: Adapt based on audience (e.g., warmer variation for artists, technical for investors)
- **Tone**: Match slogan tone to communication style (formal vs. conversational)

---

## Logo + Slogan Format

When displaying logo with slogan:

```
[Logo Image]

Empowering Artists. Protecting the Planet.

[Links: Unsubscribe | Email Preferences | Support]
```

**Note**: Do NOT include "MSC & Co |" before the slogan when logo is present. The logo already represents the brand name.
