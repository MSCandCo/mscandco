**1.1 Complete Assignment**

The Employee hereby agrees and acknowledges that:

(a) All work product, inventions, discoveries, improvements, innovations, designs, developments, methods, modifications, copyrightable works, and trade secrets (collectively "**IP**") conceived, developed, reduced to practice, or created by the Employee, either alone or jointly with others:

   (i) During the term of employment/engagement, OR
   (ii) Using any Company resources, facilities, materials, or Confidential Information, OR
   (iii) Relating to the Company's actual or demonstrably anticipated business, research, or development

shall be the **sole and exclusive property of AUDIOMSC LTD**.

(b) This includes but is not limited to:
   - **Software code** (source code, object code, scripts, algorithms)
   - **Documentation** (technical specs, user guides, API documentation)
   - **Business processes** and methodologies
   - **Ideas and concepts** related to music distribution, AI integration, or platform development
   - **Improvements** to existing Company systems (including MSC & Co platform, MCP server, Apollo Intelligence)
   - **Data structures** (including music taxonomy, enum systems, validation frameworks)
   - **User interfaces** and designs
   - **Database schemas** and architectures

**1.2 Moral Rights Waiver**

The Employee hereby irrevocably waives all moral rights (as defined under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988) in all IP created under this Agreement, including:
   - The right to be identified as the author
   - The right to object to derogatory treatment
   - The right to object to false attribution

**1.3 Future Assignments**

The Employee agrees to execute any further documents necessary to perfect the Company's ownership of all IP, including but not limited to:
   - Patent applications and assignments

   - Any other IP transfer documents
