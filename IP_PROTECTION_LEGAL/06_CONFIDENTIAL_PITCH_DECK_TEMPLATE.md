## PRESENTATION TIPS

**DO:**
- Practice your pitch 10+ times before presenting
- Customize slides for each investor (add their portfolio companies, interests)
- Keep it under 20 minutes (10-12 slides is ideal)
- Focus on the problem, solution, and traction
- Be ready to demo the platform live
- Have financial model ready for deep-dive questions

**DON'T:**
- Send deck before NDA is signed
- Read slides word-for-word
- Go over 30 minutes total (including Q&A)
- Over-promise on projections
- Share trade secret details (taxonomy structure, prompt engineering) unless necessary
